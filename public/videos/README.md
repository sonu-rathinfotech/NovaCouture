# Hero video

Put one file here, named exactly:

    hero-bg.mp4

The home page picks it up on its own — no code change. Until then the hero
shows the still photograph, which is the design, not a fallback.

## What to choose

- **5–10 seconds**, looping without an obvious cut
- **Silent.** It is muted anyway; audio is dead weight in the file
- **Landscape**, 1920×1080 is plenty. 4K is wasted here
- **Under about 3 MB.** Past that the download costs more than the movement
  is worth, especially on mobile data
- **Slow movement.** The headline sits on top of it and has to stay readable
- **Dark or evenly lit on the left**, where the text sits

## What not to choose

- **AI-generated jewellery.** Coverr's jewellery section is largely AI —
  23 of the clips on one page. The pieces in those clips do not exist and
  often have impossible details. For a real jeweller that misrepresents
  the craft, which is worse than a plain photograph.
- **Licensed stock** (Getty, Shutterstock). These files ship inside VK's
  website, and VK is who receives the letter.
- **A recognisable face**, unless the clip is clearly model-released. It
  reads as a VK campaign.

daylight. It will beat any stock clip, because it is actually theirs.
